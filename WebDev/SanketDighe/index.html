About Me

Two Decades of Designing, Building & Managing Landmark Structures

Since 2003 I have worked across every stage of the construction life-cycle — feasibility, structural design, tendering, site execution, quality control and project handover. My work spans high-rise residential towers, commercial complexes, IT parks, bridges, flyovers, highways, water infrastructure and heavy industrial facilities across India and the Middle East.

Today I lead Dighe Structural Consultants, a Pune-based practice offering structural design, project management and structural audit services. My approach combines rigorous engineering, value engineering to control cost, and an uncompromising focus on safety and code compliance (IS 456, IS 1893, IS 800, IRC & ACI standards).

What I Do

Civil Engineering Services

End-to-end engineering expertise, from the first soil test to the final occupancy certificate.

Structural Design

RCC, steel and composite design for buildings up to 45 storeys, with seismic and wind analysis as per IS & international codes.

Project Management

Planning, scheduling (Primavera P6 / MS Project), contractor coordination, cost control and on-time delivery.

Bridges & Highways

Design and supervision of PSC & RCC bridges, flyovers, ROBs, culverts and highway pavements as per IRC & MoRTH.

Structural Audit & Retrofitting

NDT testing, condition assessment, structural stability certificates and strengthening of distressed buildings.

Estimation & BOQ

Detailed quantity take-off, rate analysis, bill of quantities, tender documentation and contractor bill certification.

Geotechnical & Foundations

Soil investigation review, pile and raft foundation design, retaining walls, slope stability and ground improvement.

Water & Environmental

Water treatment plants, sewage treatment plants, ESRs, storm-water drainage and rain-water harvesting systems.

Quality & Site Supervision

Third-party quality audits, material testing, site safety compliance and Proof Consultant services for authorities.
